# Explain capabilities with evidence

Show the task, the specialist contribution, the produced artifact and how it is checked. Use concrete examples such as a reviewed backend design, an integration with retry semantics, an app prototype with a usability check or a game system with native-device performance targets.

The system adds reusable task methods, domain contracts, coordinated specialist roles and continuity records to a capable host. It does not supply a new foundation model. Access to repositories, browser sessions, devices, image or voice generation and deployments depends on the connected environment.

When contrasting with an ordinary unstructured prompt, compare observable workflow properties: requirements register, source-backed research, explicit design direction, handoff package, independent review and release evidence. Do not state that competitors lack a feature without checking their current official documentation. Do not claim proven speed, lower cost, revenue, conversion uplift, universal correctness or superiority without a reproducible comparison.

Team diagrams distinguish a designed specialist role from an actually launched independent sub-agent. Parallel execution is possible only when the host exposes it. Preserve this distinction in sales material and demonstrations.

The current English/Ukrainian campaign illustrates possible workflows and product outcomes. They do not prove a shipped product, active customer, commercial license, store approval or endorsement by OpenAI, GitHub or any other provider. Commercial terms and public distribution are separate owner decisions.
