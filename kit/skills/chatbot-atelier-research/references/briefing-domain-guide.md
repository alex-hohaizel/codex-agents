# Domain briefing and resumption in ChatBot Atelier

The [topic catalog](../assets/briefing-topics.json) is a starting map for the eleven actual entry points. Add a material topic discovered in the intent, code, source or dependency to the brief with reason, answer owner and downstream stage. Choose the topics for this task. An incomplete catalog leaves a gap.

Restore current intent, decisions, source, config and contracts, observed state and permissions. Prefill known answers with source, version and date. Distinguish the user's request from quotations, examples and hypotheses. For an existing bot, map the impact and keep approved capabilities.

Resolve the current stage and material downstream dependencies. Start with the job and audience, channels and identity, complete journey and active systems, rights and consent, data and observed effects, experience and resources, then acceptance and handoff. Conversation, knowledge, integrations and media can be clarified in parallel with a shared contract owner. Activate payments, retrieval, voice, games and initiative only where needed. Give a reason for applicability. Leave material unknowns open. Ask the next short question that affects a decision and reassess dependencies when answered.

Present useful options and a recommendation with its basis. Verify current platform, provider, cost and availability through primary documentation or actual probes. State what remains unknown about demand or capacity. «Не знаю» sends an expert to research alternatives and return a recommendation with its strongest objection. Keep agreed scope, complexity, channels, quality, budget and billing intact. The owner decides intent and material tradeoffs. The expert supplies observable facts and technical alternatives. Honor the bounds of explicit delegation.

At C1 agree the experience architecture: actors and location, full journey, systems, sources of truth, audience and consent, memory, effect lifecycle, channels and commitments. A numeric T parameter can be deferred only if no material choice is concealed. Record its range or invariant, verification method, owner and gate before the first dependent action. A backoff value can be T. Authority to retry external effects and behavior under uncertainty belong in C1. Resolve unknowns that change channel, architecture, permission, cost, quality, acceptance or the target outcome before approval of execution.

BRIEF (БРИФ) produces a reviewable map of decisions, applicability, deferred work, sources, conflicts and acceptance. Write the execution plan from that map. RESUME (ВІДНОВИ) restores map and current state, validates affected sources and reopens changed or material unknown topics. A critical gap blocks its own dependencies. Continue independent reading and research. Live effects, private consent, delivery and human naturalness need their own evidence.
