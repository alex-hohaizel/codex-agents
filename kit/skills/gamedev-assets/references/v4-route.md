# Asset production and semantic integration 4.0

## Inputs and inventory

Start from T contracts or an authorized narrow spike, exact model, state and event IDs, accepted originals, camera, input and phone framing, budgets, rights and target profiles. Derive required states, variants, clips, cues, screens, locales, and music transitions from agreed scope. Keep them all in the inventory.

## Workflow

1. Bind the output to its operation ID, ToolCapabilityRecord and ProductionRecipe. Verify actual schema, catalog and parameter bindings. Verify editable layers, rig, MIDI and stems, and native export through actual operations.
2. Choose the required representation: raster and frames, cutouts and skeletons, vector, or 2.5D and 3D. Record source, edit, export, import and consumer steps, versions, rights and limits, expected outputs, checks, repair, stop and cost.
3. For each transfer record SemanticTransformation: units, axes, scale, pivot, timebase, root motion, contact markers, color, alpha, materials, audio sample rate, channels, loops and tails, preserved properties, intentional losses and validators. Verify both format and meaning.
4. Produce actual files and preserve source and output hashes. For stochastic providers keep available parameters, model, job and seed. Claim bitwise regeneration only where supported. Reconcile unknown jobs first.
5. Verify that the source reopens and exports, and lint the import. Inspect silhouette, deformation, foot and weapon contact, cue clarity, full UI and locale states, and listen to mix and loops. Track format checks separately from review on the medium.
6. Assemble the actual scene with the build: input and camera, physics and hit windows, animation, VFX, SFX and haptics, resources, rewards and save. Exercise applicable normal, peak, failure, cancel, death and return states, lower tiers and sensory equivalents.
7. Record AssemblyEvidence for exact scene, build, config and inputs, observed modalities and open required proofs. Verify integrated contact in the actual build separately from standalone clip review.

## Domain requirements

| Asset class | Production and proof |
|---|---|
| 2D | Layer and pose registration, silhouettes, pivot, frame duration, atlas padding and bleed, filter, alpha and color. Check contact and cancellation in scene. |
| 3D | Editable mesh, hidden surfaces, topology, UV and PBR, units and axes, skeleton, bind and weights, stress poses, attachments, retarget and root motion policy, LOD and collision budget, actual import. |
| Death, crash and part break | Applicable alive, disabled, downed, dead and cleanup states, break cues, transform authority across controller, ragdoll and respawn, bounded debris. Gameplay owns outcomes. |
| UI and locales | Applicable empty, error, pending, owned, expired and restore, string IDs, plurals, placeholders and context, fonts, glyphs and RTL. Keep pricing and cancellation visible. |
| Music | Motifs, states, tempo and meter, actual editable sessions and stems where required, aligned loop boundaries and tails, transitions, runtime mix, voices, and listening. |
| SFX and haptics | Bind confirmed simulation event, material, part, force and outcome to cue variants, priority, dedupe, start, update and end. Provide mute and haptic-free equivalents and independent physical-device proof. |

Immediate hit feedback follows authoritative simulation and stays independent of disk or cloud. Wait for the applicable durability or verification boundary before presenting purchase, reward or victory success. Keep reward and damage authority outside animation and audio callbacks.

## Coverage and repair

For every required state provide actual output or justified intentional reuse or silence. Cache by semantic input and recipe, model and importer versions. Rebuild affected derivatives after changes and preserve approved originals. After two equivalent repair failures, change diagnosis or route. Route missing rig capability explicitly.

## Output

Deliver sources, exports and catalog, operation, recipe, transformation and attempt references, runtime consumer mapping, hashes, versions and rights, actual import, assembly, listening, viewing and device evidence, coverage denominator, gaps, owners and closure criteria. The receiver can reproduce the supported route without chat history. Track working native gameplay independently from planning and generated imagery.
