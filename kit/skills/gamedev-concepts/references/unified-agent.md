# Game Atelier · Concepts in the director-led workflow

> For new 4.0 results apply the [Game Model workflow](game-model-workflow.md), [model format](model-format.md) and relevant domain modules. Close each due gate with its required evidence.

## Intake and reconciliation

1. In a director-led workflow, take the task from `gamedev-director` or recover it through director intake. Record exact request and source, project and revision, authorized scope, required outcome, exclusions, budget and consent, stop boundary and next gate. Distinguish «продовжуй» (“continue”), a saved favorite, recommendation, draft and actual acceptance. Preserve the observed intent state.
2. Read the actual checkpoint, project files, `.gameatelier/orchestrator/state.json`, requirement registry, knowledge and lessons, decisions and relevant upstream bytes and hashes. Reconcile current requirements and decisions with sources and preserve them. Check the runtime ledger's current format and toolkit marker. Check archive paths, links, limits and manifest before import and keep supplied scripts inert. Preserve version, master and approval history. On resume reconcile actual job, provider or store state before retrying.
3. For each new or changed requirement retain stable ID, verbatim source, intent, stage, dependencies, artifact and evidence references, status, owner and closure criterion. Carry all current musts across a new request. `not_applicable` needs a domain reason and scope. Missing modalities and failed musts remain open regardless of aggregate score.
4. Record project knowledge or lessons with source, scope, confidence, applicable stages and dependency impact. Reconcile them with requirements, decisions and hashes before dependent work. Open a targeted issue for a conflict. Invalidate only affected outputs and reviews and preserve global skills, authority and accepted goals.
5. Present an unresolved material choice with ID, scope, two or three feasible options, effects, costs and rights, a grounded recommendation and affected IDs. Wait for the decision before dependent work and reuse valid existing consent. Complete independent authorized work so the decision can address a concrete result.

Use the [experience contract](experience-contract.md) and [systemic contract](systemic-design.md) along with domain references. Keep artistic intent, implementation parity, device evidence and user acceptance distinct. Trace actions through input, rule, motion and contact, animation and VFX, audio and music, haptics, effects and next decision. Preserve predictive, confirmation, error and recovery cues, two-thumb patterns, accessibility, and parity without sound or vibration as applicable. Choose style, engine, backend and commerce per project. Where broad practical reach and 60 FPS were selected, measure against the accepted budgets.

## Actual capabilities and authority

Verify the specific operation, credential scope, environment, rights, output format, cost and smoke result. Use sufficient available tools, CLI and skills. Give a task-local agent IDs, exact inputs and hashes, owned paths, limits and stop. The runtime handles state and gates. Verify any LLM, connector or persistent service separately. Independent reviewers must be different actual actors and staffed proportionately to risk. Record author checks as such.

Account, setup, upload, store and configuration actions can use a permitted connector, API, CLI or browser within actual consent. Confirm the exact action rather than inheriting the historic «власник робить setup» (“owner does the setup”) as a blanket rule. For MFA, personal legal or signature decisions, physical hardware or missing permissions, prepare an owner handoff with the blocked operation and reason, ready inputs and hashes, actual environment and verified URL, exact launch, click or command steps, expected result, secret-free return, verification and next move. Complete available authorized work first. Spending, public or destructive effects need exact current authority. Prepare the permitted local package while a decision remains open. Reconcile an unknown external outcome before retrying.

## Return and pause

At the task boundary return input and output hashes, actual artifacts, requirement-to-specification, data, source, asset, runtime, QA and production evidence, decisions, lessons, independent verdict, missing IDs with owner and closure criterion, and next route. Observe experience and revenue independently from structural validation. Keep workflow repair in its authorized scope.

`pause now` stops new dispatch. Safely stop the current operation or preserve its actual job ID and state. `after artifact` and `after stage` stop at the named boundary. Checkpoint with a readable brief, completed and current jobs, pending choices and evidence, exact next action and environment, and return instructions. Honor the user's stop. Resume from actual files and external state and check locks. Determine the next applicable skill from evidence. Only create recurring checks under actual authority with an available scheduler. Otherwise provide a specific manual check or runbook.

## Domain task contract

**Inputs** — Current studio revision and hash, actual research and locked intent. Preserve the scope of «visual previews», «audit», «saved favorites» or «продовжити R4» (“continue R4”). Candidate count follows finite scope and real diversity. Use `4×100` only for an explicitly selected search mode. A stop-search or concept selection needs its actual user source. Saved TatterJack favorites do not select a game. Sketch previews are C0. Candidates presented as ready for final selection need complete C1. T is the next stage for a game selected for production.

**Work** — Each sketch connects play, capability growth and potential optional purchase value. Demonstrate depth by changing decisions across systems. Include small and large concepts across relevant genres. Specify mastery, run, build, metagame, world and content structure, later reasons to replay or return, and end of launch content. Choose sports and other genre systems for their own needs.

| Discriminating check | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Live input and progression | Compare the same situation with input now, later or absent. A new capability changes an old task, trajectory, timing or route. Return a causal card, model or available prototype with raw output. Physical touch needs a device playtest. |
| Art and sensory identity | Compare directions within intent. Fantasy, pacing and mechanics inform camera, shape, material, light, UI, type, motion, audio and haptics. Preserve exact originals and knowns and unknowns. Mark actual auditions or motion samples where exercised. |
| Economy from the start | Trace desire, trial, earned route, optional purchase, repeat use and next goal. Record exact contents, eligible moment, reason and trial, earned pace, cohort effect, recurrence, production cost, cumulative access and power, paid dominance counterexample, and no-offer and meaningful alternatives. |
| Demand and feasibility | Current primary sources, concrete production route and native-slice test plan. Separate competitor success and simulation from this game's observed retention or profit. |

Compare kits, content, art ensembles, passes, resources or acceleration according to the design. Offer value can come from collection or an alternate play style and needs meaningful repeat use. Paid randomness, spending and material implementation choices need their own decisions.

**Output and acceptance** — Label C0 or deliver complete C1 in scope. Include comparison, originals and hashes, causal progression and economy models, art, audio and input feasibility, and validation gaps. Each must has a mechanism, strongest objection, evidence or limit, and next discriminating test. A failed must stays visible.

**Return** — The director presents a recommendation for user choice. Send the complete accepted concept to preproduction only after actual selection. Continued search or the 12-preview stop stays in concepts with a checkpoint. Assign unknown reverse views, rigs, stems and physics an owner and due stage.
