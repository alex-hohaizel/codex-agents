## Construct a causal game before illustration

A C0 preview shows a labelled sketch and the scoped risk it investigates. Every finished candidate offered for choice needs a complete C1 with its action fantasy, representative loop, mistakes and recovery, development, environment, economy, accessibility and production route. T remains the next gate for the chosen game. Read the [route contract](references/v4-route.md) and bind passport, cards, proposed assets and comparison to the same Game Model.

For each meaningful action write a context and state, player intent, input and timing, costs, limits, response, counterplay, and next state. Compare at least two causal situations where the preferred action changes. Try removing a rule or resource, and compare the strongest simple policy. Describe tools, player/actor attributes, role perception and memory, telegraphs, environment and encounter interactions, counter-strategy, failure/recovery and persistence where applicable. Use relevant noncombat challenges for noncombat games. Validate live control and feedback for the game's promised interaction. Extra HP, recolor, seeds or buttons alone do not add decisions.

Map location topology, routes, cues, encounters, access conditions, procedural/fairness constraints, and persistent consequences. Define mastery, run growth, permanent access or collection where appropriate. Demonstrate early, middle and late changes in player capability. Link rewards to earned actions, grant, use and the next decision. Preserve meaningful revisit choices, known or explicit unknown probability, prerequisites, and limits on repetitive content. Define a finite launch inventory and authorable content grammar. Show learning through safe try, feedback, transfer to an independent situation, failure/retry and recovery after absence. Keep settings, accessibility and state transitions in the full journey.

Use primary data for viable economic choices, player proposition, earned and paid options, reward and currency sources/sinks, constraints, price and display authority, fixed/recurring/expiry, and cumulative influence. Build credible no-offer/free or premium baselines appropriate to the model. Check decline, trial, purchase, effect, restore, refund and failure pathways. Respect any approved alternative experience in the same playthrough, as well as cumulative access and the rule against implicit refills or resets on switch. Separate scalable financial hypotheses from actual market evidence. Confirmation of a concept does not authorize paid operations.

## Prove the promise and hand it off

Compare performance on equivalent representative inputs with build/device IDs, measured metric, threshold and uncertainty. State whether it was a model, proxy, playable prototype, native run or physical device. Use a falsifiable loop hypothesis and define the cheapest real next proof if the target device is unavailable. Reject unsupported promotion based on menu or synthetic results.

Bind camera projection and tracking, distance, normal/peak framing, character and world scale, aspect, occlusion, HUD, accessibility, motion and sensory signals to the action. Use feasible rights/cost/source/export/import and model/rig/contact/scene budgets. Preserve actual distinction through shape, silhouette, material, light, interface, movement and audio. Inspect every generated frame against these contracts before showing it, recording hash, reviewer and deviations. Preserve exact chosen source and scope. Actual motion/input/audio/haptic and source-to-runtime parity are due in the representative slice. Generated images and stated FPS supply only proposal evidence.

Read the current `scripts/game_model.py --help` and `concept_gate.py` route. Model validation uses stage `concept` and the handoff pipeline uses `concepts`. Use independent causal, camera, creative, completeness, feasibility and portfolio review. Keep must failures visible and use the chosen Game Model revision, source hashes and exact owner selection for the handoff to preproduction. Return project-relative sources, lawful studio payload, frozen/open decisions, proof, risks, and next action. Keep an honest bounded repair path if none meet the criteria.
