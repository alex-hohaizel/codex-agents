# Preproduction contract T for Game Model 4.0

## Inputs and authority

Accept the selected C1 revision and hash, accepted originals and decisions, current budgets and platforms, and authorized scope. C1 defines the product. T resolves behavioral and production choices needed for implementation. Keep favorites separate from the selected game. An early spike needs its own bounded experimental contract.

## Specify every active system

For each applicable track among the 20, define purpose, owner and consumer, state reads and writes, rules and dependencies, launch catalogue or grammar, progression, UI and camera and audio, authority and saves, budget, invariants, oracle, and due proof. Continue independent branches around a blocker. Define behavior before integrating any dependent consumer.

1. **Rule Contract.** Stable ID and revision, typed units and domain and precision, initial state, guards, function, table, curve, state machine or solver configuration, update and priority and tie-break, capture time, effects, presentation and save, golden examples. A tunable parameter includes initial value, range, goal, and change procedure.
2. **Damage, physics, and lifecycle.** Trace intent, contact, eligibility, channels, modifier and defense order, barrier/HP/parts, status and reaction, and terminal objective, credit and reward. Separate delivery, channel, material, and status. Define action/target/window or effect/tick deduplication, snapshot versus live input, damage-over-time schedule and expiry, random streams and roll consumption, and recursion guards. Convert contact force to game damage with explicit unit-safe rules.
3. **Failure.** Define applicable alive/downed/disabled/dead/despawn/respawn, simultaneous lethal/phase/stagger/objective, cleanup for projectile, damage-over-time, slot and collider, lost and retained state, cost, retry and recovery. Distinguish immediate feedback on simulation commit, durable reward or spend or victory, and cloud acknowledgment.
4. **AI, world, and narrative.** Specify knowledge channel, memory TTL and confidence, decision and action traces, encounter legality, boss phases and priority, topology revisions, navigation and visibility invalidation, and story flags. Generation needs executable constraints, a repair budget, and fallback. Separate difficulty settings and progression.
5. **Catalogue.** Complete launch inventory or generator, families, compatibility, slots and tags and caps, source and chance and pity and guarantee, upgrade and build and respec and repair. Check legal and forbidden combinations, strongest simple strategy, combined paid portfolio, and capability with actual opportunity.
6. **Experience, controls, and craft.** Detail back, cancel, empty, error and commit, pointer ownership and recognition and traces, camera arbitration, learning diagnosis and transfer and return, and localization. Map functional states to editable sources, clips, cues, screens, and variants. Pin timing, units, axes, color, loop, markers, and alternative channels.
7. **Persistence.** Complete State Catalogue, schema and envelope, consistency group, atomic generations or transactions, dirty window, migration, merge, delete and retention, golden saves and faults. Distinguish platform identity, cloud owner, store account and game profile, and verify cloud sync, OS backup and purchase restoration separately.
8. **Runtime and production.** Use idiomatic module interfaces, pinned engine and exporter and platform adapters, validated data mapping, applicability of the 45 operations, recipes and transformations, actual attempts, and assembly evidence. Add backend or a DSL only when domain requirements call for them.

## Complete the handoff

| Output | Required contents |
|---|---|
| GDD and TDD projections | Versioned canonical IDs, states and exceptions, algorithms, order, timing, and units. Use the canonical authority. |
| Data and content | Complete launch rows, curves, and graphs or generator with constraints and fallback, with semantic verification |
| Production | State coverage for assets and audio and UI and locale, source and rights, exact operations, recipes, bindings, consumers and budgets |
| Test package | Independent oracles, intermediate calculation traces, boundary and failure and combination cases, golden saves and input traces, and due engine, native, and human checks |
| Receiver acknowledgment | Assets or Build verifies a fresh handoff, runs permitted examples, and identifies exact missing inputs and owners |

Verify actual dependencies beyond valid JSON, formulas, and review records. Wheel damage may change handling and routes and generation, input and camera, presentation, repair and economy, learning and save, and tests. Derive only real edges.

## Counterexamples

Absent rounding or capture or stack order, keys behind their own locks, simultaneous boss and player death depending on iteration order, max or union duplicating spent resources during cloud merge, two rescues spending twice, a reward animation granting ownership, GLB without contact and scale semantics, and a TDD without exhaustion. Every case needs an expected trace or defined rejection.

Before scaling production, verify the representative native slice with required evidence. Keep missing device proof open. Assess enjoyment, comfort, and 60 FPS separately from schema or contract validation. Transfer exact hashes, actual evidence, pending proofs, and the authorized next action.
