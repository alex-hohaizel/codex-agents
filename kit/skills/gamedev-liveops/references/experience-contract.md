## Priority, applicability, and evidence coverage

Before dependent work, reconcile intent, current decisions and requirements, lessons, package revisions and hashes. Record authority and execution priority separately. Respect host requirements and the current owner scope. An applicable owner decision governs generic skill advice. Present equally authoritative conflicting requirements and realistic options before dependent work.

| Priority | Closure |
|---|---|
| `must` and `blocking=true` | Hard requirement in scope. It has its own acceptance check. Failure or blockage stops the dependent gate even with excellent art or strong revenue assumptions. |
| `must` and `blocking=false` | Mandatory work due at a later named stage. Keep it pending at this stage and blocking when due. |
| `should` | Domain quality goal. When unmet, record the trade-off, effect, owner, and review. |
| `explore` | A testable hypothesis or option. Selection and any spending require appropriate authority. |

Prefer causal comparisons, evidence, scope and unresolved risk. Measure quantities such as frame time, memory, earning pace, input latency, cost, and unlock-time distribution with declared units and methods. Derive target and tolerance from the game. Pass each must separately from aggregates.

For every requirement, record ID, `source_user_requirement_ids`, exact source and interpretation, priority and blocking, owner, scope and dependencies, obligations by stage, artifact and evidence IDs and pinned hashes, acceptance method, status, and closure reviewer, decision, and date. Break compound requirements into checks and close the parent when all applicable due parts pass. Keep baseline musts when shortening intake.

Derive applicability from platform, device class, audience, mode, medium, feature, channel, and stage. N/A needs rationale, exact excluded scope, authority, and reopening condition. For example, a confirmed local-only offline game can exclude account recovery. A later cloud-save choice reopens it. Missing devices, tools, or budget are pending or blocked. During concepts, record future device proof as `pending`, `due_at=representative_slice`, `blocking_here=false`, with the concept evidence tracked separately.

For a project lesson, record source, scope, confidence, applicability, and impact. A change to a lesson, master, economy, rules, or toolchain invalidates only affected dependencies and reviews. Updating global skills or authority requires its own scope. Verify structure, semantics, artistic result, and actual feel through their own methods.

## Seed and whole-experience graph

Every seed includes a concrete action, contact or outcome and next decision, development of player capabilities and world, a reason to repeat or return, optional potential purchase value, and its own artistic promise. A cheap written seed needs causal description and direction. Before illustrated selection, each candidate needs a systemic passport with concrete UX, resources, alternative experiences, and a feasible production profile. Production assets and measured conversion belong to later claims.

Build `experience_graph` from input and action to rule and state and contact, multimodal response, cost and reward and world change, new choice, and save, recovery and return. For each node and edge, record purpose, prerequisites, state change, timing, player-readable cues, failure and recovery, progression and commerce links, content dependencies, owner, and due evidence. Include menus, discovery, results, offer and decline, settings, and interruptions. Identify causal changes between systems, such as a surface changing trajectory, a newly available move opening a known route, an ally changing timing, or a tool giving a new solution to an old problem.

Show the main use of mastery, session and run arcs, builds and playstyles, metagame and world development, and content grammar within the genre. Progression changes actions, contexts, objectives, or consequences. Use inventory, crafting, simulation, and resets only where they fit. In a sport, race, rhythm, or arcade game, techniques, arenas, rules, opponents, routes, and interactions may provide progression. Give an explicit N/A rationale for unused mechanics while preserving meaningful development.

Map unlocks and dependencies, early/mid/late objectives, sources and sinks, persistent and reset scope, trade-offs, recovery and catch-up, and a viable free path. Show an example of a new capability changing a familiar problem. For replay, establish alternative decisions, finite launch capacity, reasons for long-term return, and a feasible pipeline for new content. For procedural work, specify grammar, authored units, constraints, solvability and fairness fixtures, algorithm and content versions, and seeds. Verify that new seeds cause meaningful decision changes. Record return after multiple sessions only when observed.

For key loops, specify predictive, confirmation, error, and recovery cues in applicable visual, motion, audio, and haptic channels. Document what players see, hear, and feel before, at contact, and after the outcome, including UI and music transitions. Check two-thumb behavior, handedness where supported, occlusion, safe areas, color and contrast and typography, reduced motion, alternate inputs, mute and haptics-off, and audio interruption. Preserve information, timing, and play with disabled channels. Physical sensation can differ.

## Artistic direction across layers

Candidate directions can differ in camera, proportion, shape, material, lighting, motion, typography, and sound in ways that support the fantasy and input. Use 2D, illustrated layers, 2.5D, 3D, or another method when suitable. Explain influences through principles and the game's own expression. Respect rights and existing intellectual property.

Write an `art_promise` describing intended experience, motifs and contrasts, distinctive visual and audible choices, and what would break that identity. Prove coherence across layers and contexts, including a menu at rest, errors, contact, late world, and store, alongside hero imagery.

For each layer, record intent, unknowns, owner, and due proof early. Deliver production bytes at the applicable stage.

## Intent lock and production gate

Track `artistic_intent`, `implementation_parity`, `device_proof`, and `user_acceptance` as separate claims. Keep favorite, selected direction, accepted frame or motion or build, and publish permission separate. A studio master governs the approved studio scope.

Preserve each accepted original's bytes, SHA-256, media properties, source and provenance, rights and license, authority, date, and scope. Use the original master byte-for-byte. A derivative has its own recipe and source and output hashes. Keep the master immutable. A required change needs a new revision and side-by-side comparison.

A frame leaves hidden geometry and back views, topology, rig and weights, collision and movement, lettering and font binaries, stems and mix, and haptic feel open. For each missing detail, note impact, owner, proof or decision, and due gate. Develop details within current creative authority. For material changes to identity, behavior, or quality, show a representative sample and obtain a decision before dependent production.

Before scaling a batch or implementation, complete `representative_slice`. Choose the smallest end-to-end scope that covers risky art, controls, contact, audio, UI, commerce, and progression dependencies. Assets and Build can work together after preproduction.

1. Pin the canonical reference, state, camera, aspect, seed and time, acceptance parameters, and allowable deltas before comparison.
2. Verify the route, including available tools, editable sources, licensed fonts and audio and assets, export, import and assembly, pinned engine and toolchain, resources and budget. Observe the riskiest transitions.
3. Build a playable native slice with production source lineage. Exercise frames, motion and contact, gameplay audio, controls and haptics, and applicable menus, UI, decline, and reward transitions in the actual runtime.
4. Capture interactions, audition and haptics, and performance on the scoped representative target. If a required device is missing, identify the blocked gate and provide an executable handoff. The mass production gate stays closed.
5. Independently verify applicable musts, rights and budget feasibility, types of evidence, deviations, and unknowns. Obtain owner acceptance of any material art and feel still open. Transfer exact slice hashes and approved batch scope.

An overlay or controlled pixel comparison supports perceptual review, motion, audition, and contact evidence. Account for hardware differences. If the intended route cannot be proven, try another viable local route, then report the concrete blocker and handoff or propose a material change. Preserve accepted quality and identity. Use a simple production technique when it can reproduce the accepted result.

Changes to a master, renderer or camera, shared shader or rig, input or contact timing, mix, or economy reopen the affected slice evidence. A narrow unrelated typo does not reopen the slice. Derive `mass_production_permission` from current evidence, review, scoped acceptance, and hashes.

## Commerce within the desired experience

Start from a reason to try and use the offer repeatedly. Value can come from a new action, context or role, an expressive design or ensemble, ownership and collection, content, convenience, or pace. Avoid manufactured frustration and decide which part of the experience can be optional. Cosmetics or functional value can each be appropriate. Add models and currencies only when they support the game.

From the first seed, map desire, discovery, trial, earned route, optional purchase or decline, grant and ownership, repeat use, and new meaningful goal. At concept this is a hypothesis. Preproduction specifies state and data contracts. Build and QA verify actual behavior. Live operations observes use, value, and cohorts. Evaluate applicable items, tools, characters, playstyles, expansions, passes, resources, acceleration, and art ensembles. Record why each model belongs or is excluded.

For each offer, pin contents, version, entitlement, eligible time and cohort and why it appears, a real trial or a meaningful demonstration, earned path and pace and assumptions, price authority and currency and market, recurrence and duration and caps and expiry, decline and no-offer path, discovery and reuse, next goal, production and service costs, grant, save, restore, refund, revoke, pending, and error behavior. If no equivalent can be earned, describe the paid content boundary and full alternative experience honestly. Paid randomness, ads, subscription, paid acceleration, backend, and spending each require the relevant authority before implementation.

Map every currency and resource source, sink, exchange, cap, rounding, expiry, and authority to action, unlock, offer, and persistence. Give every currency a distinct purpose and account for complexity. Explain what merging currencies would change. Verify account, server, payment, and store authority from actual sources. Close MONEY-01 with purchase receipts, idempotent grant, restoration, and refund checks.

Model combined effects for a no-offer control, engaged nonpayers, different payers, and applicable new and returning players, skill levels, and markets. Match content, skill, and time assumptions. Include trial and free acquisition, full owned collections, stacked boosts and repeat purchases, pass timing, interactions and synergies, and simultaneous offers. Measure range of access and playstyles, time to capability, time and cost to goal, challenge and failure and recovery, power and dominance, social or competitive effects, enjoyment and offer load, and regret and decline where data exists. Document gaps, ranges, and sensitivity. Test collective advantage in context even when individual items have equal DPS.

Separate one-time and recurring gross revenue, refunds and fees and net revenue, variable service cost, and finite production and fixed cost. A recurring assumption needs an actual recurrence mechanism. Ground conversion, eCPM, LTV, and retention in the game's observations. Scenario results can reject an unsuitable model. Actual demand and profitability require their own evidence.

Base market potential, replay, willingness to pay, and profit on actual evidence. Team size, candidate count, and aggregate scores do not establish quality. Match checks to uncertainty, consequences, and stage. Use a counterexample or discriminating test to decide a specific claim.
