# Adaptive briefing and resume for Agent Foundry

Recognize the existing Ukrainian aliases `БРИФ` and `ВІДНОВИ` for briefing and resuming. The [topic catalog](../assets/briefing-topics.json) maps known questions. Use shared topics, current-stage topics and early downstream dependencies; do not read the entire catalog as a questionnaire or claim exhaustiveness. Maintain a project-owned record for each applicable topic: applicability and rationale, answers and exact sources, unknowns or conflicts, owner, affected IDs, due gate, and how to close it. Give newly discovered material topics a stable ID and the same fields; extend the catalog if useful. Missing access means blocked.

Read the request, current state, accepted originals, pinned sources, decisions, and supplied packages first. Reuse known answers with provenance and scope. Resume checks freshness, unresolved effects, active stops, and changed dependencies, preserves accepted decisions, and reopens affected questions only. A local repair does not restart the full process.

Separate the full goal, launch and probe scopes, and unacceptable reductions. Production order alone does not reduce ambition. Do not invent caps on hours or functions or pick input methods, platforms, monetization, or upgrade routes without evidence. When resources fall short of required quality, research alternatives and surface the material choice.

Choose the next unresolved question that changes the goal, behavior, architecture, rights, cost, risk, or acceptance, beginning with its prerequisites. Prepare real options, consequences, and a recommendation based on project criteria. Do not invent market data, demand, tool capabilities, current prices, or permissions. Accept free text and an owner's uncertainty. For an uncertain owner answer, research, compare, recommend, and present a meaningful counterargument. Uncertainty does not create consent. Specialists resolve verifiable facts within scope; users own the intent and material tradeoffs. Explicit delegation does not expand authority.

After each answer update applicability and dependencies, close resolved questions, and add new material implications. Group questions by decision coherence and the user's ability to answer. Open a future topic now if it can change the current choice. The actual brief contains only pertinent questions even when the catalog is large.

Use `criticality` to organize work: material for consequential choices, technical for specialist work, future for later parameters or proof. Investigate material effects regardless of the label. Use `answer_owner` to distinguish user intent from specialist facts. A delegated answer needs an actual assignment and a returned result. Asking about acceptance is not approval.

Before approval of execution, close all known material unknowns within scope and relevant early downstream consequences. At C1 define architecture, promise, and core behavior. Defer numeric T tuning or later exact inputs only with a baseline and range or invariant, owner, method or experiment, exact due gate, acceptance, affected IDs, and reopening condition. An undefined core rule is not a tunable setting. Give a dependent consumer precise rules before execution. Device, human and production proof remain pending until their actual gate. Structural PASS does not prove domain quality, rights, installation, or a live outcome.

The catalog has selectors `intake`, `research`, `architecture`, `blueprint`, `build`, `verify`, `install`, `refine`, `operations`, and `resume`, all using stable skill `agent-foundry`. These are Foundry briefing modes. Determine the target's actual capability, phase and component DAG from the assignment.

Keep the constructor, generated agent, and final result separate. Before deciding to build a new agent, inventory the current host, other available hosts, skill sources, prior packages, and existing specialists. Verify actual source, version, trigger, and capabilities. An unavailable host does not prove an agent is absent. Compare reuse, configuration, targeted extension, delegation, and new creation, and recommend a feasible route. In refinement repeat this only for the affected component and preserve current capabilities.

Follow actual work and raw cases → domain, methods, states and exceptions → full capability passport → standalone or family design and component boundaries → production, tools, memory, team and evidence → versioned readable passport and operating scenario. Close material construction questions before target build. Mission, competencies, core rules and authority cannot remain TBD; a future numeric parameter may have a stated range and due phase. Distinguish M0 sketch, M1 complete passport and M2 detailed production handoff.

Cover inputs, actions, outputs, scenario variants, owners, actual operations and recipes, rights and costs, memory and knowledge freshness, privacy, independent tests, host installation and launch/return, support, upgrade, recovery and exit where applicable. Add public identity, market, payment, RAG, server, app or bot for a domain need. If an app, chatbot or reusable skill is required, find the current specialist and hand off bounded scope with common intent and requirement IDs and one accountable owner. Reuse existing discovery.

Build expresses the domain method in self-contained contracts. Verify with raw forward tasks that do not supply the answer, exact coverage, and negative cases. Actual independence needs actor and output provenance. For installation, distinguish prepared package, saved state, discovery, and observed first use. Verify read-back. Construction consent is not blanket authority for future product work or effects. Preserve explicit current authority for several scopes together once granted. For operations and resume, check real jobs, unknown external outcomes, budgets, leases and stop conditions before retry.
