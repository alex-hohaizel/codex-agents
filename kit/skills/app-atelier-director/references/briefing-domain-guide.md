# Adaptive briefing and resumption (`БРИФ` / `ВІДНОВИ`) in App Atelier

The [topics catalog](../assets/briefing-topics.json) maps known questions. Select questions for the current stage and early downstream decisions. The catalog is neither a script to read in order nor a completeness guarantee. Maintain a project-owned record for each topic: applicability and reason, answers and exact sources, unknowns or conflicts, owner, affected IDs, due gate and closure method. Add a materially new topic under a stable ID with the same fields and extend the catalog when needed. Missing access blocks the affected topic.

Read the actual request, current state, accepted originals, source versions and hashes, decisions and provided packages. Populate answers with provenance and applicability limits. Reuse current answers. `ВІДНОВИ` checks freshness, unresolved effects, an active stop and changed dependencies. It preserves accepted work and reopens only affected questions. Continue a local repair without repeating the whole product cycle.

Identify the full target, bounded launch and probe scope, and unacceptable simplifications. Production sequencing does not itself reduce ambition. Do not impose arbitrary hours, feature count, input mode, target platform, monetization or upgrade paths. When resources and desired quality conflict, research feasible options and return the concrete material decision.

Choose the next unresolved question affecting goal, behavior, architecture, rights, cost, risk or acceptance. Resolve dependencies before details. Before a material decision, prepare real alternatives, consequences and a recommendation against project criteria. Do not invent market data, demand, capabilities, rates or permissions. Accept free-text answers and «не знаю». Treat «Не знаю» as an invitation to research, compare, recommend and present a counterargument. Uncertainty does not grant consent. Technical specialists establish verifiable facts within scope, while the user sets intent and chooses material tradeoffs. Explicit delegation does not authorize a broader action.

After each answer, update applicability and dependencies, close resolved questions and add new material issues. Size a round by related decisions and what the user can answer. Keep future topics visible now if the answer changes the current decision. A brief selects relevant questions from the full catalog.

Use `criticality` to sequence a material choice, technical research, or future parameter or proof. The label does not remove material analysis. `answer_owner` separates user intent from expert findings. Delegated work needs an actual assignment and returned result. Acceptance questions do not establish approval by themselves.

Before approving an execution script, resolve all known applicable material unknowns in scope and early downstream consequences. Establish architecture, promise and core behavior at C1. Numerical T tuning and future exact inputs can be deferred with a baseline or range, or an invariant, plus owner, method or experiment, exact due gate, acceptance, affected IDs and a reopen condition. A missing core rule is not a tunable parameter. Provide enough precise rules before dependent work. Future device, human or production proof stays pending until due. A structural pass does not prove domain quality, permission, installation or a live effect.

The eleven selector keys `director`, `research`, `concepts`, `specification`, `experience`, `build`, `support`, `qa`, `release`, `growth` and `operations` correspond to the actual stable `app-atelier-*` skills. Route a local task to the competence it needs without requiring the full sequence.

Early dependencies run from a real need and present audience through checked alternatives and willingness to use or pay, a coherent concept and repeated value, data/auth/platform/offline/commerce/support, UX and production, integration/QA/release, then growth and operations. An early release preflight and acquisition plan can change the concept before significant build. Choose purchase, subscription, advertising and launch scale from the actual goal.

App value research distinguishes user, buyer, interest, actual behavior and payment. Verify an existing audience, and record zero audience without assuming an available channel. A concept requires a causal user, payment, operations and acquisition path, explicit manual dependencies and grounded economics. Record a sketch at its actual maturity.

For specification and experience, connect applicable states, clients, accessibility, localization, auth, persistence and sync, recovery and conditional billing/support. Exact backoff can be a T choice. Guest, account and organization access, offline promises, conflict resolution and paid entitlements are early architectural decisions. Start build with the riskiest representative end-to-end path on declared clients. Verify transformation from source to editable master/export, integration and actual use before expanding. A separate support bot has a component owner, bounded handoff and stable specialist route.

QA pins exact outputs and distinguishes evidence modes. Record independent review only for independent work. Release distinguishes prepared candidate, owner readiness, exact permitted effect, submission or approval, and actual availability. Growth stays within actual authorized channels and resources. Recurring work requires a running mechanism. Operations covers costs, incidents, upgrades, export/deletion and retirement when applicable.

Handle material departures through a bounded delta and routine repair in existing scope. Current contracts are [workflow](workflow.md), [app-depth](app-depth.md), [app-system](app-system-contract.md) and [acceptance](acceptance.md).
