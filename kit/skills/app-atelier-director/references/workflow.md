# Integration workflow

## Inputs and current scope

Accept plain-language requests, current project records, packets, repository and version, and actual observations. Distinguish changing an agent from executing product work. Before execution, capture the current brief with goal, audience, scope and stopping condition, priorities, known constraints, resources, decisions, and dependencies. Importing or approving a packet preserves completed work and grants only the permissions explicitly conveyed.

## Delivery stages

1. Intake. Verify the actual profile, state, rights, capabilities, resources, and execution scope. If an owner-approved starter profile exists, carry it into a project-local source with provenance and a digest. Keep the shared skill independent of project state.
2. Research. Route the market tracks from the profile. Begin with a short map and deepen according to the evidence and resource plan. Obtain the owner's choice of direction before proceeding.
3. Concepts. Cover the complete user, payment, operations, and acquisition journeys and their economics. Check manual dependencies, competitive scenarios, and assumptions. The owner selects a concept or authorizes bounded prototypes.
4. Specification, experience, growth planning, and release feasibility. Align the scenario, design, data, auth, billing, support, cost, and constraints with a realistic store, SDK, budget, and testing route. The owner accepts material scope, design, and budget.
5. Representative slice, build, and support. Prove a full, high-risk scenario before scaling. Assign leads distinct paths, API contracts, and outputs. Mark unavailable platform checks explicitly.
6. Independent QA. Pin every due requirement and artifact. Turn each finding into a specific repair. If an independent reviewer is unavailable, record the self-check and the pending gate.
7. Prepare release, confirm owner readiness and authority for the exact effect, invoke the real tool, and record the receipt and read-back. Store submission, approval, and production availability are distinct states.
8. Post-release. Run authorized activation, metrics, and monitoring. For a request or incident, assess impact and route targeted specification, build, QA, and release. Retirement has its own data, export, deletion, and cost contract.

## Task and resource contract

For every delegation, record goal, input digests, assigned requirement IDs, owner, paths, outputs and interfaces, evidence and acceptance, time, token, call and spending caps, stopping condition, and reviewer. Assign each path one writer at a time. A worktree shares any production database or external account unless the actual environment isolates it. The root integrator owns changes to shared state and approval records.

Size the roles to the deliverable. An author cannot independently review their own work. When subagents are unavailable, proceed sequentially where possible and report the true independence status. After two failed repairs with the same result, diagnose before expanding the team or rounds.

## Memory and corrections

Trace source → requirement → task → artifact → evidence → review → next action. Record a lesson's confidence, scope, applicability, and impact. A lesson conveys no new permission. When a source, version, or permission changes, mark dependent checks stale. Retain exact originals and receipts alongside the current brief. On resume, inspect external state before retrying an unknown effect.

## Visible result and proportionality

Apply the [adaptive workflow](adaptive-work.md). Show the integrated requirements and dependencies, why the team is sufficient, integration decisions, whole-product acceptance state, and next valid action. Distinguish work actually researched or produced, inherited current decisions, and future results with their due gates. Scale depth and specialist roles to uncertainty, consequence, and coupling. Use a local repair for a local change.

For material gaps, choose the relevant [research, synthesis, or reconciliation mode](research-and-synthesis.md). Review completeness against original intent, adequacy of the composition, shared contracts, and quality of the whole deliverable. Review raw sources for requirements omitted during synthesis. Verify reviewer competence beyond actor ID and keep structural checks at their actual evidence level. Preserve project context, current approvals, and unaffected results.
