# Complete product, operations and evidence-led evolution

## Applicability, inputs and owned outputs

Use for every game, including offline, premium and offer-free games. Read launch scope, active domain contracts, accepted masters, target platforms and devices, actual capabilities, rights and budget. Deliver the product lifecycle map, finite content and cadence plan, save, update and support contracts, measurement and experiment plans, release and rollback criteria, and checkpoint and return bundle. Use the [lifecycle template](../../assets/domain-templates/lifecycle-measurement.template.json) within the existing authority and infrastructure.

## Full experience and finite launch scope

Describe acquisition and the store promise, installation and first opening, learning and the first satisfying loop, early, middle and late progression, completion and postgame, return after absence, and support, updates and offboarding where applicable. Record actual content units and families, meaningful differences, authored vocabulary, composition and prohibited combinations, novelty choices, milestones, ending and replay value. Check that the promised game extends beyond the demonstrated scene and menu.

For each promise map the rule, data, asset, runtime and user-state consumers and when evidence is due. Resolve excessive scope through specific tradeoffs before production commitment. Retain accepted requirements when using an MVP label. A small game can concentrate depth, and additional scenes should contribute distinct decisions. Define a complete product with a finite acceptance boundary.

## Save, offline, interruption and updates

Specify authoritative state, save boundaries, schema and version, atomic commit, backup and integrity, corrupt-save recovery, migration and rollback, inventory, unlocks and world state, settings, learning and session resume. For offline-first designs specify permitted actions, clock changes and claims. Define sync, conflict handling and account merge when a backend applies. Add accounts or servers only for actual product needs. Reconcile an unknown purchase with its authoritative source before changing local balances.

For applicable suspend, resume, process termination, connectivity loss, audio focus, notifications and deep links, specify player-visible outcomes. On return, show the current goal, equipment and resource state, reward once, and an available continuation. Check content, config and save compatibility, safe rollout and rollback, rights and SDK changes, and fair handling of paid entitlements. Restoring an old build must not repeat external effects.

Support requires reproduction, build and device IDs, a minimal privacy-safe trace, selected commerce receipt and state lookup, triage, severity, owner, player message, recovery and escalation. Collect only necessary data. For service retirement or final content, where relevant, define understandable access, save and entitlement policies with a supportable infrastructure horizon.

## Growth, retention and organic sharing

A return hypothesis identifies concrete player value such as new decisions, mastery, an expected unlock or reward, discovery, a voluntary shared goal or another meaningful outcome. Validate interest independently of push notifications, streaks and artificial scarcity. For sharing define a standalone moment or result, voluntary capture and share, viewer value, attribution when possible, and a path to the game that avoids spam. Include content and support cost even when the advertising budget is zero.

For each metric record name, event, schema and version, numerator and denominator, population or cohort, calendar and time zone, observation window, exclusion and missing-data policy, uncertainty, privacy and consent, and the decision it supports. Distinguish install, first open, activation, completed loop, return, engaged time, payer and actual receipt. Keep D1/D7 targets separate from observations. State limits from sample size and selection bias.

Player tests record the question, recruitment and participant limits, scenarios, assistance rules, observed behavior and errors, actual builds and devices, anonymized outcomes and residual risks. Use AI critique and simulation for test design or analysis and collect human observations from actual participants. Set experiment assignment and control, hypothesis, primary and guardrail measures, exposure, duration rationale, stop and rollback, and decision rule before viewing results. Attribute causal change only where the design supports it.

## Content cadence and adaptation

New content changes decisions or interactions. Specify vocabulary growth, production and QA costs, reuse, compatibility, overlapping seasons or expiry where applicable, exhaustion horizon and ownership. A complete premium game may use a support and update lifecycle without continuous live service.

Trace each new requirement, defect or observation through provenance, affected IDs, causal impact, scoped work, actual output and evidence, review and closure. Preserve accepted decisions and masters. Treat lessons from one game as scoped evidence. Assess agent improvements separately from the current game's requirements, decisions and authority.

Reopen checks for transitive impact. A camera change affects input, readability, AI and art. A progression price affects rewards, commerce and pacing. A shared rig or shader affects dependent assets and captures. Save schema changes affect migrations and resume. Reuse past evidence with identical relevant hashes and circumstances. After two failed repairs with the same result, change the diagnosis.

## Stage gates, roles and continuation

Concepts need a complete launch and lifecycle arc, a causal retention or growth hypothesis, and honest production constraints. Preproduction needs typed contracts, data, spec_refs and executable risk models. The slice needs actual source, native runtime and device observation, save and resume, and peak behavior. Beta requires real people and cohorts within its claims. Release requires current independent review, actual builds and devices, critical money, save, privacy and authorization checks, current platform requirements and exact authority for external operations. Live operations need actual telemetry, support and provider outcomes.

The producer integrates scopes and dependencies. Domain authors prepare contracts. Runtime, platform and operations owners implement or operate actual behavior. Independent QA verifies relevant claims. People evaluate human experience. Record who performed each role and scope staffing to actual need and available slots.

At pause or transfer between Work and Codex retain current source, version and hash, accepted choices and favorites, finished and current work, unknown external outcomes, exact boundary, next action, and a verifiable launch and return package. Concurrent authors of one project or production resource need explicit ownership or locking. Verify isolation directly. Skill installation leaves chat history and local toolchains in their own hosts. Background monitoring or schedules require actual authorized creation.
