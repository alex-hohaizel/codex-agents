2. Read the actual checkpoint, project files, `.gameatelier/orchestrator/state.json`, requirement registry, knowledge and lessons, decisions and relevant upstream bytes and hashes. Reconcile current requirements and decisions with sources and preserve them. Check the runtime ledger's current format and toolkit marker. Check archive paths, links, limits and manifest before import and keep supplied scripts inert. Preserve version, master and approval history. On resume reconcile actual job, provider or store state before retrying.
3. For each new or changed requirement retain stable ID, verbatim source, intent, stage, dependencies, artifact and evidence references, status, owner and closure criterion. Carry all current musts across a new request. `not_applicable` needs a domain reason and scope. Missing modalities and failed musts remain open regardless of aggregate score.
4. Record project knowledge or lessons with source, scope, confidence, applicable stages and dependency impact. Reconcile them with requirements, decisions and hashes before dependent work. Open a targeted issue for a conflict. Invalidate only affected outputs and reviews and preserve global skills, authority and accepted goals.
5. Present an unresolved material choice with ID, scope, two or three feasible options, effects, costs and rights, a grounded recommendation and affected IDs. Wait for the decision before dependent work and reuse valid existing consent. Complete independent authorized work so the decision can address a concrete result.

Use the [experience contract](experience-contract.md) and [systemic contract](systemic-design.md) along with domain references. Keep artistic intent, implementation parity, device evidence and user acceptance distinct. Trace actions through input, rule, motion and contact, animation and VFX, audio and music, haptics, effects and next decision. Preserve predictive, confirmation, error and recovery cues, two-thumb patterns, accessibility, and parity without sound or vibration as applicable. Choose style, engine, backend and commerce per project. Where broad practical reach and 60 FPS were selected, measure against the accepted budgets.

Reconcile an unknown external outcome before retrying.

## Domain task contract

**Inputs** — Exact independently verified candidate, source, build, config, content, catalog, economy and save versions, studio and legal state, rights and privacy inventory, device verdicts, and consent and rollout limits. Keep build ready, QA accepted, upload authority, store approval and actual availability separate.

**Work** — Recheck current official store, SDK, payment, privacy and test requirements, available capability and permissions. Perform supported account, config, catalog, upload, submission and rollout operations within exact current scope. Reuse a valid grant and rely on actual MFA, legal and payout confirmation. Complete available binaries, metadata, field mappings, rollback and smoke plan before a pending material decision.

| Discriminating check | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Exact candidate parity | The released binary and config hash match the accepted candidate or receive targeted new QA. Store captures come from actual runtime with exact studio master, fonts and rights. Keep advertising art and gameplay capture distinguished. |
| Input, sensory and device behavior | Release smoke exercises critical controls, contact, feedback, audio focus, mute, no haptics and accessibility, and long-session and minimum-device checks for agreed budgets. Verify controls, art, sound and FPS after signing, export or optimization. |
| Commercial correctness | Native product mapping matches exact contents, price source, eligibility, trial, earned route, recurrence, restore and refund. Actual catalog preserves the accepted free path, cumulative access and power and paid dominance limits. Record sandbox evidence independently from live billing readiness. |
| Account, privacy and legal | Observe public developer and seller IDs, agreements, tax and payout state, actual SDK data behavior and disclosures. Keep pending IDs pending. Verify specific legal decisions separately from store rules. |
| Effect and recovery | Record provider receipt, time, target, channel, version and state, availability and smoke, and halt, rollback or forward fix. Reconcile uncertain uploads before retry. Verify actual recovery support for installed mobile binaries. |

An owner handoff specifies a verified App Store or Play page with ready field values, files and hashes, required MFA or personal agreement step, expected receipt and safe return. Missing macOS or signing needs an exact build or export task and inputs. A new paid backend, subscription, paid randomness or pricing model needs a scope decision.

**Output and acceptance** — Bind prepared, submitted, in-review, approved, testable or live status to actual evidence. Provide manifest, store metadata, product and privacy snapshots, sanitized receipts, rollout and stop plan, symbols, migrations, runbooks and gaps. When final publication authority is pending, complete the reviewable local package and name the exact effect that still needs authority.

**Return** — Director hands off to live operations after actual release or beta handoff. Route rejection, signing, binary, commerce or art findings to the responsible owner and targeted QA. Monitoring needs an exact authorized scheduler action. A release claim needs evidence for the game itself.
