# Stage contract for chatbot-atelier-conversation

## Visible result and applicability

Determine the task scope and a sufficient method using [adaptive work](adaptive-work.md). Apply [focused research and synthesis](research-and-synthesis.md) at any stage when it is relevant. The agreed project profile defines active functions, platforms and persona. Preserve established IDs, permissions, accepted results and full intent. Bind reviews to the exact observed source and version. A local repair uses the affected route and its applicable acceptance requirements.

Show the conversation states, languages, tone, addressees, real examples and contrasting evaluations. Keep model scoring and human acceptance of naturalness separate. Retain agreed conversation and experience requirements through earlier technical work.

## Connect wording to the right person and state

Restore the bot's mission, audience, persona, supported languages and cultural register. Check recipient, referent, reply/topic, direct or ambient intent, pause, and freshness. Leave human-to-human requests with their intended recipient. Where a phrase such as “the next one” refers to two pending actions, ask one meaningful question and perform no ambiguous action. Keep a clear new request visible when initiative is paused.

Use a natural voice with relevant warmth, humor or stance. Avoid recurring offers, therapeutic templates, invented biography, and implementation language in the user experience. Offer the simplest complete useful response first and at most one relevant extension. Preserve the full scope of complex tasks.

## Make dialogue and interaction honest

Bind tool phrases, examples, onboarding, help, progress, errors, and final responses to actual operation capability and state. “Prepared,” “queued,” “started,” “completed,” and “delivered” each require their own evidence. For a failed or unknown effect, report the actual status and useful next action. Use the existing controller and authoritative owner.

Bind start, continue, cancel, retry and detail controls to relevant decision facts, state/version, expiry, and visible result. Check long text splitting, locale and display on the actual target. Group actions have optional join/exit, each participant's own consent, stable roster, late join policy, private instructions, and a meaningful first move. One person's consent or refusal controls only their applicable scope.

Evaluate frozen old/new contexts across direct, indirect, human-to-human, playful or emotional, pause, ambiguous, failure/repair, and memory return. Use minimal pairs and 1-, 5-, and 20-turn synthetic continuations with real phrasing per supported language. Count false speech, silence, wrong recipient, unnecessary questions and task loss with denominators. Blind voluntary human A/B allows ties and both-bad outcomes. Keep naturalness, privacy, consent, actual effects, integration acceptance, and deployed conversation observations distinct.

## Handoff

Provide stage ID, requirement and source IDs, exact versions and hashes, applicability and reason, typed rules, states and cross-system dependencies, capabilities and authority, operation contracts, recipes and effective parameters, actual attempts and job IDs, outputs and consumers, evidence and limits, negative cases, unresolved items, receiving owner and next acceptance check. The receiver can verify the finding without chat history. Return incompatible input or an infeasible requirement to the appropriate upstream owner.

## Criteria and completion

Check applicability for these criterion IDs first: natural_voice, context_relevance, proportional_depth, simple_first, recovery_empathy, multiuser_fairness, interaction_continuity. Use all 24 baseline IDs for the impact map. Record active, carried or out of scope with reasons based on the current profile and accepted scope. Retain all approved requirements. For each active requirement record an owner, artifact, positive and negative checks, evidence kind, verdict, strongest objection and limits. Unknown evidence for a critical claim blocks the affected readiness. The author and independent QA are distinct. Human or production verification requires the actual applicable observation.

Deliver the complete requested result. Track concept, specification, production handoff, integration, live observation and human evaluation independently. When two attempts yield no new evidence, change the diagnostic hypothesis. A depleted budget leaves unmet acceptance criteria visible.
