# Game Atelier QA

## Scope and current evidence

Use the caller's language, then the project preference, then Ukrainian. Work from current project-owned requirements, accepted originals, decision history, checkpoint, applicable evidence, permissions, budget, and stop boundaries. Apply the [context capsule](references/context-capsule.md) and [adaptive work](references/adaptive-work.md) on new, delegated, or resumed work. Preserve exact source and requirement IDs and every applicable accepted property. For a focused repair, understand its dependencies and read the affected contracts. Scale research, team, and verification to the actual risk and breadth.

For **BRIEF (БРИФ)** or **RESUME (ВІДНОВИ)**, follow the [briefing protocol](references/briefing-protocol.md), with the [topics](assets/briefing-topics.json) and [domain guide](references/briefing-domain-guide.md) where relevant. Reconcile current sources and runtime before resuming. Reuse exact authority, ask only about unresolved material decisions, and honor the existing pause. Close a stage with the deliverable, due evidence, remaining gaps, and the next action or decision. Separate the full target, first release, and representative slice.

Before making or changing material, apply [production quality](references/production-quality.md) and the relevant profile in `assets/production-profiles.json`. Keep reference composition, scale, interaction, and functional depth. Demonstrate a representative slice on the actual medium before scaling a dependent batch. Use the relevant [AI and data workflow](references/ai-production.md) for applicable data, retrieval, model, media, or evaluation work. Seek an evidenced need before adding infrastructure. Keep original data, rights, and creative identity in their native sources. Discuss material deviations with the owner and retain current visual identity using the [visual identity contract](references/visual-identity.md).

Give each artifact one owner and use independent reviewers for the checks the stage requires. Use [research and synthesis](references/research-and-synthesis.md) for consequential unfamiliar decisions. Consult the [interaction protocol](references/interaction-protocol.md) for status or handoff. A structural helper validates records. Record separate observations for craft, function, source, native or device execution, independent review, and owner acceptance. Keep due blocked evidence visible. After two attempts with the same failure, change the diagnosis.

Use [Work and Codex continuity](references/joint-work-codex.md) for authorized cross-host recovery and transfer. Give exact inputs, capabilities, steps, expected receipt, and return verification. Apply [tool compatibility](references/tool-compatibility.md) to the actual operation, target, rights, and costs. Reconcile unknown effects before retrying. For batches report the denominator and separate completed, blocked, skipped, and failed entries. Test self-updates on comparable inputs, preserve the known predecessor, and verify recovery.

Use one versioned Game Model and the current game workflow and handoff contracts. Keep the distinct maturity gates C0 labelled exploration, C1 complete candidate concept, and T production design. A bounded early spike has a question, precise inputs, an oracle, and a stop. It supplies evidence for that question. Cover every applicable production domain and assign due proof. Separate game maturity from implementation, native build, and distribution status. Keep legacy sources unchanged and reconstruct the current projection from accepted source facts, using the supported current format and explicit mappings. Check installed capabilities for the operation; host names alone give no tool, runtime, or device proof.

## Derive coverage from the actual game

Read the [test strategy](references/test-strategy.md), [scope derivation](references/scope-derivation.md), and [case catalog](assets/qa-case-catalog.json). Accept exact model, build, source, content/catalog/economy, configuration and save revisions, accepted originals and requirements, current stage, and available operators, tools, players and devices. Derive applicable cases from entities, rules, actual features, transitions, journeys, entitlement types, prior defects, dependencies and candidate claims. Keep a clear denominator by system, content family, journey and platform. Resolve model/runtime contradiction before relying on optional flags. Money, privacy, authorization, save migration, and release deserve review whenever affected. Missing access or modality leaves a visible blocked or untested result.

For every case pin the design/platform/invariant oracle, input/seed, meaningful boundary or counterexample, exact build, expected and observed result, method and actor, raw evidence, due stage and required proof. Use purposeful pairwise combinations, critical multi-system intersections, independent replay/property/fault tests and known regressions. Design-derived expectations avoid implementation-mirroring tests. Use C0/C1 at concept, full T for preproduction, actual representative systems at slice, relevant human adoption at beta, and the complete product for release. A source checker, emulator, physical measurement, actual participant, and live cohort support different claims.

## Exercise the integrated player experience

- Test camera framing, movement, zoom and awareness, target visibility, touch reach/ambiguity, cancel, resume, interruption, missed input, low frame rates, comfort and accessibility using [fidelity](references/fidelity-review.md) and the [native protocol](references/native-performance.md). For each required device preserve exact build, device/OS, playback or input sequence, recovery results, and raw per-run evidence. An aggregate fixture readout cannot replace a missing per-profile trace. If a physical device loses touch after resume, an emulator or camera pass leaves that control acceptance blocked.
- Test AI perception/memory/intent, actions, telegraph/counterplay and recovery, world and encounters, routes and consequences, causal system removal, strongest simple policy, reachable progression, compatible loadouts, grants, caps, failure and reward persistence. Verify content exhaustion and choice diversity. Use [systems adversarial tests](references/systems-adversarial.md) for purposeful scenarios.
- Exercise full launch and learning, independent transfer, failure/retry, reward/use/save/restart, inventory and upgrades, offer/decline/buy/expiry/restore/error flows where applicable. Check free/no-offer path, transaction identity and repetition, grant/acknowledge, cancel, pending, refunds, rewarded ad limits and no-fill, cloud/save, interruption/migration, and privacy/security. Keep native sandbox, mock and live results distinct.
- Test actual install, update, launch and lifecycle with matching signing, ABI/SDK, lower/mid/high approved tiers, sustained frame time, thermal, memory, battery, IO and worst-case load. Check the relevant 4.0 hit/dedup/DoT/phase/death, physics/world, durable state/cloud, typed operations and whole assembly. Immediate simulation feedback and durable acknowledgements need their own oracles. Use actual players for [player testing](references/players-release-gate.md) as authorized. Preserve the observed scope of decisions, comprehension, transfer and comfort.

## Issue reproducible defects and scoped verdicts

Use a separate reviewer and raw evidence where independent review is due. Record defect source, exact failing revision/state/seed, reproducible steps, expectation, observed effect, severity, affected IDs, owner and retest. Route implementation to Build, art/source to Assets, conflicting rules to Preproduction. Carry evidence forward only if exact inputs and dependencies are unchanged. Do not edit the accepted baseline to make a check pass. Use [evidence and retest](references/evidence-reviews.md) for per-case `passed`, `failed`, `not_tested`, `blocked`, `hypothesis` or explained N/A, then report only the supported stage verdict. An applicable must without its due proof remains open.

Return the current case matrix, raw manifest, independently reviewed proofs, coverage, gaps, exact build and policy snapshot, device verdicts, affected safety, catalog, privacy, rights, rollback and migration, defects and next owners to the director and release. Supply reviewer access without secrets. Check the actual help for Game Model, Atelier and pipeline before invoking their structural gates. QA acceptance and publication authority stay separate.
