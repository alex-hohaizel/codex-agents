# Independent game acceptance 4.0

## Establish scope and evidence level

Pin revision, inputs, build, config, content, catalogs, save and toolchain, source requirements, model maturity C0/C1/T, and implementation stage. C1 checks the complete intent of every presented finished concept. T checks defined behavior, catalogs and production handoff. Runtime, device, human and live evidence are due at their own gates.

For each of the 20 active tracks, derive claims, dependencies, oracle source, denominator and verification method. Give N/A a game-specific rationale. Mark missing capabilities not-tested or blocked. Independent reviewers actually read or use the artifact and must be different from its authors.

Test simulation commit and durable commit independently. Ordinary hit feedback stays independent of cloud and disk. Under the defined guarantees, verify no loss or duplicate acceptance of reward, spend, or confirmed victory. Exercise actual boundaries with controlled failures.

## Verify production

For each operation examine ToolCapabilityRecord, executable recipe, semantic transfers, observed attempts and reconciliation of unknown results, and integrated evidence. Distinguish discovery, actual operation, and native integration. A failed required quality criterion stays open independently of average scores.

Have a fresh receiver reproduce the named scenario and expected results from the packet. Return missing input, rule, launch catalog, state assets or unresolved behavior to its owner. Keep QA independent of authoring missing design.

## Verdict

Per criterion report passed, failed, not_tested, blocked, hypothesis, or N/A, actual evidence, time, actor, method and inputs, independent counterexample, uncertainty, and coverage denominator. For distributions include variance, tails and thresholds. Measure TTK and human balance at their own evidence levels alongside DPS. Match perception, haptics, comfort, and retention claims with appropriate actual methods.

Retest affected dependencies after repair. Change diagnosis after two equivalent failures. Carry old proof forward only where dependencies stay valid. Preserve 3.0 history and require relevant 4.0 acceptance. Publication requires current authority separate from a QA pass.
