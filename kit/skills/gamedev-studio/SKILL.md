## Produce and verify identity

Separate creative production from account or service readiness. Give each independent worker owned inputs, targets, limits, and proof, and use a real independent reviewer for material changes. Use current primary evidence for name, rights, domain, and store questions. Show distinct, feasible style choices when the direction is open. Bind any approved choice to the exact original bytes, revision, provenance, rights and observed approval.

Record the actual form, lettering, character, proportions, texture, contour, lighting, palette, typography, clear space, target crop, minimum sizes, and permitted variations. Retain accepted master bytes. Export only the named derivatives from the lawful origin, keeping recipe and hashes. Compare each output against the master on the actual target canvas and at intended display size. Verify contrast, safe areas, legibility, transparency, color, fonts, and repeatability. A bright or legible derivative showing another mark or product identity fails the release gate. Pause the affected handoff for a material silhouette, facial, lettering, layout, or material change. Record the actual rendering or inspection scope rather than treating an image hash as visual approval.

## Prepare, observe, and transfer setup

Verify exact capability, rights and costs for developer verification, legal/display names, signing and SDK, relevant accounts, support, site/privacy, payout/tax, CI, crash analytics and only the services actually required. Prefer a sufficient route with an observed done check. Separate planned actions from performed and read-back actions. Capture dated primary source, target, preconditions, credentials boundary, owner, expected/recurring cost, steps, result, receipt, dependencies and recovery. For an owner-only legal, MFA or physical step give a verified minimal bridge and safe return proof. Unknown outcome requires reconciliation before a repeat.

Deliver an exact source/master/export, rights and usage map in the studio payload, with catalog, guide, type and provenance records, reproducible import, present/readiness evidence, and exclusions. Use `assets/studio-catalogue.template.json` and `assets/studio-setup.template.csv` as forms. Link every use in planning, assets, build, QA, and release to revision and ID. For redistributable limitations give a lawful restore route. Keep personal identifiers, secrets and private signing keys out of the bundle. Transfer package-relative paths, hashes, checkpoints, consumer steps and open blockers. Read `scripts/pipeline.py --help` for actual integrity checks. A document or hash proves only its own property.
