## Domain task contract

**Inputs** — Exact accepted T design, data, asset and studio revisions or bounded spike contract, pending decisions, requirement and evidence graph, live toolchain and target platforms. Verify production source separately from concept imagery. Give platform, engine, online, commerce or quality gaps to the owner and continue independent feasibility work.

**Work** — Bind feature IDs to canonical specifications and data, implemented source and asset bindings, native build and design-derived oracle. Carry the same rule, event and catalog identity through code, UI, audio, save and telemetry. Consume canonical numbers. Exercise a representative early slice from launch and menu through controls, full attempt, result, progression, save and return with production art, motion and contact, audio and haptics.

| Discriminating check | Evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Responsive action | Actual traces for input now, later, absent, canceled and multitouch, with contact windows, latency, low-FPS behavior and two-thumb obstruction. Align prediction, confirmation, error and recovery with rules. Use actual control for the control claim. |
| Fidelity and sensory integration | Compare target and actual in the same scene, seed, camera and state with motion and audio. Bind assets, fonts, motifs, imports and tolerances. Critical state remains understandable in mute, no-haptic, reduced-motion and unsupported modes. |
| Functional progression | Early, middle, late and return fixtures change available actions or old tasks. Content grammar, reachability, variety and caps, and mastery, run and meta persistence match data. |
| Commerce and reuse | Real UI covers desire, trial, earned route, paid option, repeated use and next goal, with exact offer, eligibility and recurrence. Include cumulative access and power simulations and no-offer and alternative cases. Separate native sandbox grant, pending, cancel, restore, refund, revoke and idempotency from mocks and live sales. |
| Native reach and performance | Actual build, install, logs, captures and profiles on selected platforms and representative lower and mid-tier devices. Where selected, verify sustained frame times, thermal, memory and named support floor for broad reach and 60 FPS. |

Missing Mac, signing or device access needs an exact handoff with Xcode sources, recipe, host and expected result. Keep Android APK and AAB, iOS simulator, archive, signed device and TestFlight claims separate. Preserve controls, assets, progression and commerce under toolchain limits. A material tradeoff needs a sample, impact and decision. Routine optimization within bounds is autonomous.

**Output and acceptance** — Complete finite agreed code, assets and data, source and toolchain locks, actual artifacts, receipts and install states per platform, runtime coverage, migrations, canonical seeds and saves, raw evidence, issues and an independent QA task. Distinguish author tests, independent acceptance and experienced play or sound.

**Return** — The director sends the exact frozen candidate to QA. Route rule, asset and identity causes to preproduction, assets and studio respectively. Version changes to binaries, data or craft, and reopen affected reviews only. Publication needs its own scope and proof.
