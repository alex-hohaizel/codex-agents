## Add meaningful product cases

- Core: useful end-to-end outcome, restart/return, invalid/empty/large input, cancel/retry/recovery.
- Data: migration, corrupt input, sync conflict, delete/revoke/export and backup restore.
- Authorization: guest, different subjects/tenants, untrusted IDs, attachments and cache, logout and expired grants.
- Payment by model: pending/success/failure, restore, duplicates and reordered events, expiry/cancel/refund/revoke and unknown outcome.
- Support: applicable adapters, distinct permissions, linking/revoke, knowledge version or injection, AI/API cap or outage and unresolved cases.
- UI: accepted masters and states, viewports, input and navigation, readability, contrast, focus, accessibility and offline degradation.
- Operations: cap, external outage/quota, actual monitoring alert, rollback compatibility and secret/log hygiene.

Scope cases by impact and applicability for small repairs.

## Verdict and gates

Every due must needs an exact subject hash, independent actor where required, method, actual proof and limitations. Document review, mocked integration and device proof have distinct scopes. An inaccessible host or device leaves its proof blocked; N/A requires a domain reason. Future proof is pending with owner and due gate. Pass counts cannot compensate for critical privacy, auth, billing, spending or release findings. Label simulator evidence and require representative physical evidence for claims depending on it. Do not bypass a store or human-testing conflict with recruiting, fabricated accounts or a silent platform reduction. Desk or synthetic results cannot establish profit or human UX. Conclude with a scoped QA verdict. Owner readiness and exact release remain separate decisions.
