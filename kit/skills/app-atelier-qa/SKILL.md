# App Atelier QA

Before a material production choice or dependent artifact, follow the [production choice contract](references/production-choices.md). Bind the current project decision, accepted master and flow, and verify the phase before using the result. Reuse verified decisions for narrow repairs.

Review the exact app, artifact, or agent behavior the task asks to verify. Keep the approved product and primary objective intact. Use the explicit output language for the current action, then the applicable project or surface preference, then English. Accept input in any language. Keep native IDs, names and approvals unchanged.

## Scope the review

- For an existing App Atelier product, identify the authoritative `.appatelier/profile.json`, `.appatelier/state.json`, original request, requirements, decisions, accepted masters, current builds and stop boundary. Read the [core contract](references/core-contract.md) and relevant [requirements](references/requirements-contract.md) when the review depends on product state. For a bounded artifact or fixture, start with the supplied contract and inputs. Missing project state blocks only the claims that depend on it.
- Pin the exact artifacts, code, data, configuration, tools and dependencies relevant to the claim. Identify the author, reviewer, authorized paths and effects, environment, consumer, budget and due gates. A reviewer must be an actual actor who did not author or change the reviewed result. A rerun by the same reviewer does not add a new independent actor. When independent review is unavailable, keep useful self-checks labeled and leave the independent gate open.
- Start from the [workflow](references/workflow.md) and [evidence contract](references/evidence-contract.md) for material QA. Use the smallest check that can falsify the claim. Preserve authority, requirements, applicable risks and pending work without restarting the product cycle. Do not create a parallel canonical register.

## Match due requirements to proof

Build coverage from requirement and original source to rule, state, interaction, artifact, method and oracle, actual observer, and due verdict. Check the relevant client and build for each row. Reconcile the original request against the register, including requirements omitted by earlier summaries. For full app acceptance, read the [app system model](references/app-system-contract.md), [cross-domain contract](references/cross-domain-core.md), [QA depth guide](references/app-depth.md), and [acceptance matrix](references/acceptance.md). Preserve existing `TEST-01` through `TEST-44` and extend them for actual product risks. The [case and evidence templates](assets/app-depth.templates.json) plan coverage and need current results for acceptance.

Use an oracle independent of the implementation for critical numerical rules, permissions and state transitions. Automate checks where the assertion is clear. Check input boundaries, forbidden transitions and recovery. Choose combinations by dependency and risk. Distinguish relevant requirements due now, work due later, and features inapplicable to the accepted product. An unavailable tool, environment, SDK or device leaves dependent proof blocked. Document the reason for any genuine inapplicability.

For a raw agent forward case, give the runner the skill, ordinary user request and minimal relevant inputs. Keep the expected answer, diagnoses, repair instructions and scoring criteria with the evaluator. Isolate cases from earlier results. Evaluate the actual outputs, tool effects and permissions against the raw task. Diagnose or replay a known failure separately. Use controlled data and authorized fixtures, without spending, production writes, publishing or private-data transfers just to demonstrate behavior.

## Test the affected product paths

- **Client and design.** For each applicable Android, iOS and web client, exercise the accepted core through success, save and resume, invalid or empty input, cancellation, interruption, retry and recovery as relevant. Record exact build, OS, browser and device. Verify the actual UI and assets against the accepted masters, including the defining mechanism, scale, composition, states, interaction and motion. Check keyboard, focus, touch, navigation, text scaling, contrast, accessibility, older supported devices, and offline or degraded behavior where required. A marketing landing page cannot satisfy a web product requirement. Apply the [production contract](references/production-quality.md) and [medium profiles](assets/production-profiles.json) when visual fidelity, asset production or assembled output matters. Establish a representative slice in the target medium before expanding the work.
- **Data and access.** Check guest, user and tenant boundaries, cross-account lookups, cache/history/files/logs, logout, consent, revoke, delete and restore. Check sync ordering, interrupted writes, timeouts, uncertain effects, backup and code/data rollback compatibility. Reconcile unknown external outcomes with the provider before any retry. A binary rollback cannot reverse a payment, external effect or incompatible schema change.
- **Commerce, ads and support.** Where applicable, exercise provider sandbox receipts and entitlement verification, restore, duplicate or reordered events, expiry, cancellation, refund and revocation. Check ad labeling, placement, interruption, caps, fallback, ad-free restore and rewards promised by the model. Verify each supported channel's public and private scopes, account linking and replay, webhook and group rejection, knowledge freshness and tone, caps and outage fallback. Use the [data and support contract](references/data-support-contract.md) for exact identity and delivery rules.
- **Performance and release prerequisites.** Measure relevant latency, resource use and errors against accepted budgets in the actual target environment. Check recurring dependencies, spending limits and outage behavior. Before a significant build and again before submission, verify current store, signing, account, testing, policy and cost feasibility. Read the [budget contract](references/budget-contract.md) and exact [tool compatibility](references/tool-compatibility.md) entry when these affect the operation.

State what each result proves. Keep document, structure, artifact identity, controlled fixture, provider sandbox, simulator or emulator, headless browser, physical device, and external read-back evidence distinct. A passing local simulator cannot supply a required physical Android receipt. State which device action and return evidence are missing. Market research, estimates, synthetic users and owner review cannot establish human usability or actual demand or revenue. Honor the project's limits on interviews, recruitment, human pilots and A/B experiments in every phase. Never evade these limits with synthetic accounts or paid testers. Analyze passive data only within the existing rights, retention and budget and explain any store-policy conflict before changing scope.

## Report and hand off

Record each finding with severity, requirement ID, exact subject and proof hashes, environment, actor, raw reproduction, observed and expected behavior, impact and next check. Identify reproduced defects separately from inconsistencies and untested concerns. The author fixes the issue. A genuinely independent reviewer reruns the failed case and affected regressions on the repaired bytes. Carry unaffected proof forward only with unchanged dependency evidence. After two equivalent unsuccessful repair attempts, change the diagnosis instead of repeating the same review.

Give a `passed`, `failed`, `blocked`, or `pending` verdict for each due requirement and the assigned scope. Include actual commands or receipts, exact evidence, scope and limitations, and the next authorized action. Keep current and deferred state readable in the canonical project. A critical due requirement lacking current actual proof prevents a release-ready verdict. Release approval and owner acceptance require their own explicit evidence. Close a terminal assignment after delivering its result.
