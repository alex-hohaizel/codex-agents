# Feasible acquisition and evidence-backed claims

Contents include channels, assets, automation, measurement, and changes.

## Verify fit before full build

Read `cross-domain-core.md` and `app-system-contract.md`. Connect user triggers, alternatives, factual differentiation, an accessible channel, landing or store flow, first useful action, reason to return, offer and cost, and support. A route described as «вірусність», SEO, ASO, or AI content still needs a mechanism. For each channel, record eligibility, access, target, source rights, mechanism, inventory, lead time, limits and cost, maintenance owner, quality criteria, risks, counterarguments, and stopping condition. Reject any route whose daily manual work violates a mandatory requirement.

## Claims and materials

Bind each promise to an exact released capability and build, scenario, source or evidence, and scope. Screenshots of released states must reflect current functionality. Creative assets preserve the product promise. Inventory launch assets, including store and landing metadata, screenshots and locales, permission and privacy descriptions, content units and update cadence, and optional share or referral assets. Track generated raster, editable source, localized copy, actual upload, and accepted publication as distinct deliverables or effects.

Start the production recipe from the released source, claim ledger, and accepted brand. Produce copy and visuals, keep editable sources, export localized variants, validate meaning, readability and rights, preview on the exact channel, submit within authority, and reconcile the receipt and read-back. Preserve feature availability, numbers, currency and billing periods, links, locale grammar, disclaimer meaning, accessibility, and brand traceability. Choose an authorized fallback or record a blocker when a generator lacks an output parameter. Preserve originals, digests, licenses, fonts, and the variant index.

## Bounded automation

Specify actual runtime and scheduler, allowed sources and data, freshness tests, bounded outputs, publication permissions, deduplication, quotas and reserved cost, owner, alerting, stop, revoke, and recovery for recurring jobs. Plan dependencies as a DAG. Run a representative dry run and failure test before scaling. Reconcile unknown provider outcomes after publication or spending timeouts before retrying. Serialize writes to shared accounts and asset paths across agents. An operating campaign requires execution evidence beyond its plan and deliverables.

Product sharing is voluntary. Sending messages to an individual's contacts requires separate, exact authority. For URLs, deep links, tokens, or referrals, define consent, attribution boundaries, TTL, abuse controls, revocation, and privacy where applicable. Make tracking visible and permitted.

## Measurement and causality

The event dictionary defines the eligible denominator, source, client, version, identity and deduplication, timestamp and timezone, event transition, retention, access, and redaction. Distinguish reach, install, activation, return, payment, provider callback, and unique person. Tie costs, revenue, and refunds to accounting periods and settlement lag. State attribution window, model, and unknowns. A before/after comparison alone supports a descriptive result. Verify telemetry consent and data gates before collection.

Before an authorized bounded experiment, define hypothesis, metric, threshold, horizon, cap, stop, and next decision criterion. Preserve any current prohibition on experiments involving people, including A/B testing and recruitment. Passive observation, synthetic checks, and owner review support narrower claims. Keep evaluation criteria fixed and let measured conversion drive profit estimates. Mark missing data as unknown.

## Acceptance and scoped changes

Planning readiness covers feasibility, material contracts, economics, and dependencies. Activation readiness also requires the exact release, accepted assets, current rights, budget and stop controls, and actual target. Record the attempt, job, receipt, read-back, and observations after an action. An independent reviewer verifies material claims, rights, and the combined commercial experience. Record author self-check separately.

A change to features, pricing, locale, store rules, or toolchain makes dependent claims, materials, and recipes stale. Repair the affected scope. A handoff to a fresh actor includes the channel plan, claim and evidence ledger, masters and variants, automation and runbook, current grants and caps, metric definitions, receipts and unknowns, and next action. On stop, preserve current jobs and effects. A new campaign requires its own authorized start.

## Visible result and proportionality

Apply the [adaptive workflow](adaptive-work.md). Show a verifiable channel, materials and claims, funnel relationships, costs, automation contract, and result data. Distinguish completed work, inherited decisions, and future results with due gates. Scale depth and specialist involvement to uncertainty, consequence, and coupling. Use a local repair for a local change.

For material gaps, use the relevant [research, synthesis, or reconciliation mode](research-and-synthesis.md). Review claim evidence, channel eligibility, resource caps, and causal interpretation. Inspect raw sources for omitted requirements. Verify reviewer competence beyond actor ID and keep structural checks at their actual evidence level. Preserve project context, current approvals, and unaffected results.
