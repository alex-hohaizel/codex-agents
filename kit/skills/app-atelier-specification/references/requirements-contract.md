# Requirements, decisions and accepted intent

Recover the exact source and verbatim requirement. Decompose a compound requirement into stable IDs. Each record contains intent, priority (`must`, `should`, `explore`), emphasis, applicability and rationale, owner, dependencies, due gate, acceptance and negative case, artifacts and evidence, and closure. Determine substantive applicability. A missing tool, device or source does not make a requirement N/A.

Read current musts and the profile for each job. A new request does not remove constraints that it does not mention. A score or rank cannot compensate for a failed must. A future-stage proof stays pending until its gate. An early concept does not require a release build to complete its own stage.

For a material choice, present concrete options, consequences, recommendation, rationale, due phase and work that can proceed independently. The owner retains the profile's choices about direction, concept, design, budget and readiness. Make other minor reversible decisions within the accepted limits.

A decision record binds the exact question, options and scope to the actual answer, its source, verbatim text and actor, and the current subject revision. A hash cannot authenticate a human's intent. Verify the actual source. Do not infer consent from silence, a recommendation, a score or an arbitrary `approved=true`.

Before an experiment or bounded test, record the metric, threshold, time horizon, cap, stop and allowed next action. A profile's prohibition on deliberate human experiments applies at all phases, including unpaid A/B tests, surveys, recruiting and pilots. Passive post-release data requires a separate data decision. It does not grant permission for experiments.

Keep exact originals and accepted design, specification and masters, with provenance, hashes and scope. A single image does not establish the entire source. Assess the impact of a changed requirement, version, fact, price or right. Reopen transitive downstream outputs and tests and retain valid evidence for unchanged dependencies. Authorize a material scope or agent capability change specifically. A product lesson does not rewrite the generic skill.

## Reconcile intent and applicability

Before closing material scope, an appropriate independent reviewer compares the original messages and sources with the requirement map using the [adaptive contract](adaptive-work.md). Distinguish obligations, questions, examples, options and assumptions. Do not turn an example into universal policy. Give the reviewer the raw sources as well as the author's list. A small reversible task may use a clearly labeled self-check. A required independent gate remains pending until a real review.

Profile constraints and accepted decisions have a source and scope. Missing information does not grant spending, experiments or an external operation. New family guidance does not replace the current project profile. A late must retains its owner, output, dependencies and due gate and stays visible after the first working slice. Close a requirement with evidence suitable for its stage. A valid structure for a partial list does not prove complete coverage of intent.
