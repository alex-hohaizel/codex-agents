# Research, synthesis and coverage

Apply these functions with the [adaptive work contract](adaptive-work.md), the applicable domain rules, and current project-owned sources. The function name alone does not establish that a separate agent ran.

## Inputs and outputs

Take the goal, task and project IDs, original sources and revisions, inherited decisions and requirements, actual tools and rights, output and acceptance, limits and return destination. Understand the whole task sufficiently to avoid conflicts with its goal, data, design, architecture, operations or authority. Identify missing competence or data and useful independent work. An unfamiliar request does not have to fit an installed family.

Return artifacts and their revisions or hashes, findings and alternatives, recommendations, limitations, unmet criteria, unresolved choices, actual actor, job and tool evidence, and the next step. Register them using the project's native ledger. Saving a document does not establish acceptance.

## Research

Derive questions from intent, uncertainty, risk and pending choices. Relevant subjects include concepts, needs and markets, domain rules, technology and patterns, data, experience or language, feasibility, implementation, incidents, metrics, support and migration. Select what matters to the task.

For a material question, record an ID, the decision or result it informs, a suitable method, primary sources with dates and versions, claims and counterevidence, missing data, confidence and limits, alternatives, recommendation, owner and due stage. Check current facts at their actual sources. An earlier green report does not prove current runtime behavior.

Search broadly enough to identify alternatives, then focus on decisive questions. Run the cheapest useful probe that distinguishes them when it answers more than another description. Keep supplier claims separate from your observations. Address conflicting and inconvenient evidence and make unavailable sources visible. Stop when evidence is sufficient for the current decision or the applicable budget is reached. Return the remaining uncertainty and next experiment.

## Synthesis

Recover goals, actors, outcomes, components, interfaces, data, state transitions, failures, observability, cost, authority and decisions. Reconcile messages, documents, repositories and local copies, schemas, tools, metrics, logs and real runtime. Preserve origin, currency and scope. Map source to rule, component, result, and metric or evidence. Investigate missing connections rather than repeating a declared diagram.

For a new architecture or rewrite, compare feasible choices against the full applicable intent, real feasibility, migration, compatibility, failure modes, ownership, cost and maintenance. Separate the team and agent design from the product design. Scope a small repair to its sufficient impact and verification. Return explicit decisions or ADRs, integrated dependencies, a migration and verification plan, and the condition and date of any deferred question. New facts update affected conclusions within the common source of truth.

## Coverage

Read the original request, current requirements, scope and decisions, and actual outputs. Independent material review uses original sources and evidence without a prepared gold answer. State the reviewer, method, competence and oracle, or the missing specialist or evidence.

Trace each obligation, question, emphasis and negative example to its analysis, decision and deliverable. Look for omissions, examples mistaken for defaults, unsupported assumptions, lost late requirements, stale bytes, invalid exclusions, excessive approval or delegation, unsafe reuse, hidden consequences, and unsupported claims. Verify results in their required medium.

Use `covered|gap|deferred_with_basis|unknown` for material requirements. Include exact source and output references, findings and a targeted repair. Evidence retains immutable source and artifact hashes. After bytes change, repeat the affected review rather than refreshing an old proof's hash. Keep structural verdicts, actual review and self-check separate. Check affected regressions after repair.

## Coordination and closure

Choose roles according to independent questions, context size, domain skill and acceptance. One actor can own closely related results. The author cannot provide an independent acceptance result. Assign bounded inputs, outputs, authority and stop rules. Record actual execution and each actor's contribution. Keep one writer per shared state. Review the impact of changed inputs before integration.

For blockers preserve completed, current and pending status. Continue authorized independent work. Give people exact next actions, target location, text or command, expected receipt and return path. Verify uncertain effects before retry. Preserve the required quality level when a ready-made skill is absent. Check the current goal, due obligations and relevant source revisions before closing.
