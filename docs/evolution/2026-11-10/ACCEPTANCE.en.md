# How revision0.2 will be accepted

**29 planned cases, zero executed, zero input-bound.** Passport: ai-data-production-evolution/0.2. [Machine register](ACCEPTANCE.json) · [Real-task protocol](REAL-TASKS.en.md) · [Українська](ACCEPTANCE.uk.md).

All24 previous IDs and their capability meanings remain. A25–A29 add actual context, reference/craft, panel, complexity-admission and strict non-regression gates. This revision authorizes documentation/publication work only; it executes no task and creates/installs no target update.

Retain35 owned reusable sources (33 core plus two existing optional) and12 existing role profiles/settings. Keep seven logical competencies; there is **zero mandatory new skill/profile**. Any later extraction requires demonstrated actual-task benefit, preserved full scope and an exactly revised approved composition before target creation/install.

The seven logical competencies are data collection, analysis, LLM/tool workflows, prediction, justified model adaptation, media production and evaluation. They describe work, not a mandatory new folder or team inventory.

After exact implementation approval, bounded isolated candidate trials/prototypes under existing owners are permitted within the approved scope to test actual benefit and preservation. A28 governs production/operational admission, merge, release, installation and activation, not permission to write an approved isolated trial. A later new installable module/role extraction still needs an exactly revised composition and approval before creating/installing that new target; distinct-output/reuse evidence can come from an existing-profile trial. This documentation-only revision creates or runs no trial.

## Rules applying to every case

- Bind raw task/reference/authority inputs and hashes, exact baseline/candidate, native state and actual model/tool/access/resource conditions before execution. Keep private source locators, chats and data outside public distributions.
- Freeze each accepted function/invariant, expected result, procedure, dependency closure, due proof and coverage denominator. An unknown is not a pass; a missing required input/capability remains blocked/open.
- Compare the same task under equal or explicitly accounted conditions. Predeclare realistic task cost/latency/resource envelopes, quality rubric, held-out/adversarial cases and stochastic repeats where needed. Do not invent an arbitrary zero-jitter demand or relax limits after seeing results.
- Any detected accepted-function loss, even noncritical, or lower required quality while adding a feature rejects the candidate. More features, a higher aggregate score or cost savings cannot compensate. Retain the baseline, diagnose/repair and retest the affected closure.
- Actual context/reference/panel gates cannot be closed by synthetic fixtures, a screenshot, source/schema checks or model turn completion. Keep functional, craft, source, runtime/device/provider, independent review and owner acceptance distinct.
- Planned execution status remains unchanged until the corresponding actual evidence is bound. Publishing these documents is not a passing model, panel, adoption or release test.
