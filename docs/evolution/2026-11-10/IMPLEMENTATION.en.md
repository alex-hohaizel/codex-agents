## Context: implemented candidate

Previously, the capsule could correctly carry the requirements supplied to it while having no way to detect an original source omitted from that projection. The new optional binding connects explicitly declared original files and native requirement registers to the task. It checks their current bytes, project root, expected IDs, mandatory status and declared literal excerpts. Missing undeclared sources and lost meaning still require independent reconciliation. Existing projects without a binding retain the old route and are labelled unverified; an explicit required binding cannot silently fall back to that route after a failure.
