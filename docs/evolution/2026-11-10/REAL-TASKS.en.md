## RT00 · Common method and evidence

1. **Freeze the real task.** Bind the original raw owner request, later steering, exact approval subject, current stage and full desired result, including deferred work. Documentation-only authority must not be expanded by an old implementation approval.
2. **Pin baseline and candidate separately.** Record actual source/build/native-state/master hashes and dependencies. An old screenshot proves captured appearance, not an unavailable old build’s identity or behavior. Do not rebind old proof to new bytes.
3. **List every accepted invariant.** For each applicable function, requirement, decision, identity property, setting, permission and unfinished/unknown action, record ID/source, owner, expected result, procedure, dependencies, due gate and proof type. A known defect is not an accepted feature: bind its intended accepted contract, reproduce/fix the defect and retain affected valid functions.
4. **Freeze a task-specific rubric.** Define function and craft before seeing results. Read actual references and their accepted/inspiration/rejected status. Extract composition/relative scale, information density, typography/material, motion, navigation, content, interaction and functional depth. A visual metaphor creates no unrequested working feature.
5. **Account for conditions.** Record actual model/version/policy, tools/SDK/export/import, host, access, source freshness, context, devices and resources. Keep conditions equal or explicitly account for differences before attributing improvement. A stronger model, new access or larger budget is not silently credited to the workflow.
6. **Predeclare realistic envelopes.** Define task cost/latency/resources, measurement method, quality thresholds and uncertainty/repeat method. Include rejected attempts, repairs, accepted-output and integration/QA cost. No arbitrary zero-jitter rule or universal numerical taste score. One noisy timing observation is not automatically a lost function; uncertainty remains not-pass. Never relax limits after results.
7. **Keep independent challenges.** Reserve held-out/adversarial cases and different-style references where relevant. Repeat stochastic work sufficiently for the predeclared decision and retain actual samples. Reduce reviewer order/author bias where possible; do not disclose the evaluator’s diagnosis/expected answer to the executor.
8. **Execute the actual named route.** Only after input binding and authority, inspect raw outputs in the actual medium/consumer, including normal, boundary, error, restart/recovery and the defined journeys. A fixture proves its boundary, not the named real task.
9. **Review what really happened.** Independent material review requires a competent actor different from the author. Record actual assignment/actor, bytes, procedure, observations and limits after work. Keep source, function, craft, integration, runtime/device/provider, independent QA and owner acceptance separate.
10. **Reject accepted regressions.** Report the complete per-item/per-invariant denominator. Any detected accepted-function loss, even noncritical, lower required quality while adding a feature, identity drift or unauthorized setting/scope change rejects the candidate. A higher aggregate score, lower cost or extra feature cannot compensate. Retain baseline, diagnose/repair and rerun affected closure. Unknown/missing/unperformed proof stays open.
