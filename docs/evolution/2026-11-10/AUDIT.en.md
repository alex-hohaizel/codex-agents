## What was inspected

The inventory covers all 33 core entry points, two optional owned sources, the 12 host-role sources, host profile, shared standards, installers/updaters, constructors, service boundaries and tracked helper surface. App/ChatBot/Game entry-point coverage is 31/31; domain-depth review concentrates on the mechanisms relevant to this request. The [component catalogue](CURRENT-SYSTEM.en.md) and [helper catalogue](HELPERS.en.md) give the paths and evidence boundaries. [REQUESTS.json](REQUESTS.json) preserves every requested capability and additional constraint.

Method: current source reading, manifest/Git inventory, targeted static analysis, comparison with installed source and current primary documentation. Parallel reviewers covered core/control, AI/data/ML and product/craft. They produced distinct source reviews; this is not three independent executions of the system. Existing historical verification was read with its original scope. This audit did not train a model, call paid APIs, scrape an external platform, deploy a product, evaluate real-user adoption or independently replay every historical test.

The private baseline found matching entry files for the 33 installed core skills and Orchestrator; the optional maintenance source was not installed. All 12 installed role files matched the canonical role sources. Entry-file comparison alone is not full-tree verification, host discovery, invocation or product acceptance. Raw paths, local state and account records are kept outside this reusable repository. Private product agents, all product chats, vendor caches and every external account were not inventoried or updated. Their native state must be checked when a particular project adopts the update.

## How the requested capabilities are covered

R01/R06/R07/R11/R15 require a complete usable product and productionization route, not a new title for the constructor. R02 requires actual collection/recovery and data quality. R03/R09 need evaluated NLP, clustering, embeddings and LLM/RAG choices. R04/R12/R16 need costed multimodal production, identity and real consumer integration. R05/R13 require predictive validation and honest business uncertainty. R14 needs actual feasible adaptation and serving proof. R08/R10/R17 span architecture, coding assistance, dynamic teams and maintainability. Each has explicit owners, stages and cases in the request/acceptance registers; none is closed by a source file alone.

The proposal is language/framework agnostic. Choosing Python for a particular model pipeline does not require rewriting a native mobile app in Python. LangGraph, LangChain, LlamaIndex, XGBoost, LightGBM, MLX and PEFT are conditional options with decision criteria. Microservices, vector databases, a fixed team and a major version are not default requirements.

## What this audit cannot certify

It does not prove future taste, domain correctness, paid-provider availability, model accuracy, access to every chat, real-user value, all target devices or every media type. A model's ability to write code on request is distinct from a reusable implemented and tested system mechanism. Reproducible cases establish practical capability; fictional years of human professional experience are never claimed.

WORK stays deferred. No existing product is migrated here. The next decision is the exact new passport; only then may its target sources be created/changed/installed. Current audit documentation may be published without calling the proposed runtime update complete.
