# Purchase and support privacy draft

The release candidate stores local test orders in an owner-only SQLite file.
The fulfillment flow needs an opaque order reference, opaque buyer reference,
edition, terms versions, product major, artifact digest, redemption token hash
and delivery receipts. No name, email, host credential, chat, or project data
is required for its controlled demonstration. Local redemption codes are
bearer access secrets: keep them in private files and never in command history,
screenshots, public source, or logs.

Before real sales, the verified seller must publish a privacy contact,
applicable legal basis, actual processors, retention/deletion periods,
tax-record obligations, and channel-specific disclosures. The test service
does not prove a production privacy program or buyer identity. Purchase
documents retained by the buyer preserve acquired rights when a store is
unavailable. The product does not erase customer work when an order status
changes.
