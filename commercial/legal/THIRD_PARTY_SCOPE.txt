Third-party scope for the commercial release candidate

This original commercial license does not replace third-party licenses.
The canonical package builder must include a file-by-file or dependency
inventory and the exact notices for the runtime actually distributed.
Preserve the Node.js LICENSE with any Node binary, as well as the notices
of runtime dependencies such as React, React DOM, Lucide and their bundled
dependencies. Preserve source and package notices when their original
files are shipped.

The original showcase has separate provenance records in marketing/campaign.
That provenance identifies source bytes; final seller approval of distribution
rights and any model/provider terms remains part of the commercial release gate.
External user-supplied template/module catalogs are not redistributed by this
product unless their rights and required notices have been reviewed.

Do not publish a customer bundle while its actual dependency notice inventory
is incomplete. A list of names alone is not the complete notice obligation.
