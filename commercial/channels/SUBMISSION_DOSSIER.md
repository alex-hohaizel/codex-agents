# Sales-channel submission dossier — draft

No store listing, campaign agreement or paid service has been submitted or
accepted. The independent buyer ZIP and offline acquired-version rights are
the full product. Narrow text exports are separate coaching samples, not the
same runtime. The owner must approve seller identity, contact, tax/payment
details, channel terms, launch-ready price and refund disclosures.

## Direct download and partner campaigns

Use the [canonical offer](../offer.json), local buyer packages, signed release
index, EN/UK sales pages, factual screenshots and demo material. Supply a
temporary test redemption order to a reviewer privately, using the seller
test flow; keep the license version, edition, seats and artifact digest pinned.
Do not put codes or buyer references in public source.

unverified. [Submission](https://help.appsumo.com/article/92-how-do-i-submit-my-product)
· [Review process](https://help.appsumo.com/article/701-how-we-vet-new-new-listing-applications)

## Text-only channel exports

The Agensi sample has one SKILL.md and no runtime dependencies. The published
packaging rules put limits on files/text and do not execute shared package files
as a local tree. Full local roles, app runtime and lifecycle are not preserved
by this export. A live listing is needed for an actual platform acceptance test.
[Packaging](https://www.agensi.io/docs/packaging)

The PromptBase sample is one self-contained SKILL.md. Current guidance does
not allow a ZIP/PDF as a normal listing and requires owner manual submission.
Its new-seller limits do not change our full-product draft prices; a sample
price is a separate proposal, pending owner approval.
[Skill format and submission](https://promptbase.com/help/selling-prompts)

MCP Market's public page offers skill upload or repository sync. The dossier
does not assume the full native setup is delivered by its plugin. Confirm
ingestion, license preservation and receipts before claiming this route live.
[Seller description](https://mcpmarket.com/sell)

## Review partner

Provide a temporary test entitlement to the exact candidate through private
delivery. Request disclosure of free access or compensation. Compensation,
affiliate percentages and any paid placement are unset until owner/channel
approval. Ask the reviewer to show the actual install and first task, name
environment limits, and disclose unresolved signing or hardware checks.
